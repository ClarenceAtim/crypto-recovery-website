Scams & persuasion fraud

Talked out of your crypto? The jargon is not your problem.

Most people who reach out were persuaded, step by step, by someone patient — or opened their wallet one morning to an empty balance. Both are the kinds of cases this service was set up to review.

  • Fake investment and trading sites that kept asking for “one more deposit”
  • Romance scams and people posing as support staff, police, or tax officials
  • A link, QR code, or fake app that silently emptied the wallet
  • Funds taken and moved on without your permission

A completed blockchain transfer cannot be reversed — anyone who says otherwise is lying. What can be done is follow where the money went and prepare evidence an exchange, the police, or a lawyer can act on. If that road is already closed, we tell you before you spend anything.

Scammed before? Read this

The second scam wears a “recovery” badge.

Fraudsters watch for victims and come back posing as the help. These are the warning signs — we do none of them.

✕They message you first on social media, Telegram, or WhatsApp.
✕They want a fee paid in crypto before doing any work.
✕They ask for your recovery phrase or ask you to “connect” your wallet.
✕They promise 100% recovery, or say they can “hack the funds back.”
✕They claim a blockchain transfer can simply be reversed.

Stolen & illicit transfers

Stolen crypto doesn't disappear. It moves — and movement leaves a trail.

Thieves eventually need an exchange, a service, or a cash-out point to spend what they took. When the path is documented clearly, that point can freeze, flag, or report the funds — and police have something concrete to open a file with.

Bring whatever you still have: a screenshot, a payment link, a chat, the name of the app. Never send passwords or recovery words — the case form will stop you if you try.

Questions

Straight answers about crypto recovery.

I forgot my recovery phrase. Is my crypto gone?

Not necessarily. If part of the phrase survives, or the wallet file or the device is still around, recovery is often possible — missing one or two words is usually workable. Tell us how many words are missing, never the words themselves. If truly nothing survives, there is nothing to test against, and we will tell you that honestly.

Can stolen crypto actually be recovered?

Sometimes — and it depends on where the money went. A transfer can't be reversed, but if funds reached an exchange or a service that can freeze them, a clear trace report gives that company and the police something to act on. If the trail is cold or the funds were cashed out, we say so before you pay anything.

I was scammed. What should I do first?

Stop sending money — any “final fee” or “tax” demanded before a withdrawal is part of the scam. Save everything: screenshots, chats, payment links, transaction IDs. Then report it here and to your local police. Speed matters because stolen funds are moved quickly.

Do you need my seed phrase or private key?

No. Never. Our case form is built to block them. Legitimate recovery work is done with you holding your keys — anyone who asks you to hand them over can take everything in seconds.

What does it cost?

The case review is free. Wallet recovery is success-fee only: you pay the agreed amount only if access is restored. Tracing and report work is quoted in writing after the free review, once we know how far the trail goes. There are no surprise fees and nothing is ever paid in crypto upfront.

I inherited a wallet and don't know what's in it. Can you help?

Yes. Bring the device, backup, or notes the person left behind. We can establish what the wallet holds and document it in a form your lawyer or executor can use. Don't try random passwords repeatedly — some wallets wipe themselves after failed attempts.
